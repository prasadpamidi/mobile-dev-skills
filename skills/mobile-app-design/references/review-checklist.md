# Mobile design review checklist

Use only the items relevant to the requested screen and supported form factors.

## Product and hierarchy

- Can someone tell where they are and what the screen is for?
- Is the main action clear and connected to the user's actual task?
- Does the visual idea come from this product's content, behavior, or brand?
- Are labels, sample values, and imagery credible and internally consistent?

## Platform and interaction

- Do navigation, back, dismiss, selection, menus, and system bars behave as expected on each target?
- Do controls work, show state, and give timely feedback?
- Are loading, empty, error, offline, permission, and success states present where needed?
- Does the keyboard avoid covering the field, action, or validation message in use?
- Are gestures discoverable and backed by an accessible alternative when needed?

## Layout and accessibility

- Do safe areas, cutouts, system bars, and persistent controls leave content usable?
- Do text and layout adapt to large system text, localization, and long labels?
- Do screen readers announce purpose, value, and state in a useful order?
- Is meaning available without color alone? Is contrast sufficient?
- Are tap targets forgiving and reachable for frequent actions?
- Does the layout respond to compact and expanded windows, orientation, or fold posture in scope?

## Finish

- Are spacing, typography, color, shape, and icon style coherent with the app?
- Is motion restrained, purposeful, and reduced when requested by system settings?
- Did a rendered review uncover clipping, overlap, wrapping, or inert controls?
- Are any intentional exceptions or unverified contexts called out in the handoff?
